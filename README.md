# Inbox Triage for Cursor

Every inbox, triaged by your assistant. Inbox Triage connects Gmail, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, iCloud and IMAP mailboxes to the agent in Cursor, and gives it the judgment around the mail: a spam and scam screen that runs as mail arrives and learns from your corrections, a daily brief of what needs you, VIP and quiet-hour rules that every connected app follows, newsletter cleanup, and a 24-hour undo on every change. Nothing is sent until you approve it in chat.

## Setup

1. Install the plugin. Cursor adds the Inbox Triage MCP server at `https://getinboxtriage.com/mcp`.
2. Click **Authorize** on the connect card. You sign in to your Inbox Triage account in the browser; no mailbox password ever passes through Cursor.
3. Connect your inboxes at [getinboxtriage.com](https://getinboxtriage.com/inboxes) if you have not already. Gmail and Microsoft use OAuth; iCloud and IMAP use an app password entered on a masked page.

That is all. No API keys, no environment variables.

## What the agent can do

- Read, search and summarize mail across every connected inbox.
- Get a brief of what needs you: VIPs waiting, replies owed, snoozes due, stale drafts.
- Review and rescue screened mail, judge the grey area with a fixed rubric, and report junk.
- Archive, trash, star, label and snooze, with one undo for 24 hours.
- Rank newsletters by noise and unsubscribe with one click where the sender supports it.
- Draft replies and send them only after you approve the exact draft in chat.

## Privacy and security

Mailbox credentials are encrypted on Inbox Triage and never shared with Cursor. The agent receives message text marked as untrusted data. See the [security page](https://getinboxtriage.com/security) and the [privacy policy](https://getinboxtriage.com/privacy).
